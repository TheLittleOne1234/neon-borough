CONTROLS

  • WASD — move on foot  |  MOUSE — aim & left-click to shoot
  • F — enter the nearest stopped vehicle (marked P if parked), or bail out instantly while driving
  • SHIFT sprint  |  CTRL (or double-tap a move key) dodge roll  |  1-8 / wheel weapons, hold Q for the wheel, R reload
  • Hold F beside a moving car to carjack it. While driving, hold fire aimed out of a side window for a one-handed drive-by.
  • W/S accelerate / reverse, A/D steer, SPACE handbrake / drift
  • P — pause / resume  |  R — restart after a run ends

CONTRACTS

The right-hand panel tracks your active contract — steal a car, chase down a glowing beacon, recover a weapon or body armor cache, wreck rival vehicles for a demolition job, or keep your head down and shake off the heat.

HEAT

Wanted stars run 1–5. Past 2 stars cops start shooting; past 3, wrecked cruisers drop armed officers; past 4, expect coordinated roadblocks and flanking units. At 5 stars the whole department turns out — cruisers are tougher and come in real numbers, so switching vehicles or losing line of sight matters.
